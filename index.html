The retailer desk
How this desk stays up to date

Once a day, an automated check tries to read product listings published on each retailer’s daily-deals page, plus Best Buy’s and Woot’s official developer APIs when a key is configured. Some retailers block automated requests or only show products inside a browser — those are shown honestly as unavailable, with a direct link instead of a guess. Nothing here is ever invented.

A listing with no stated end time is marked “End time not provided” and drops from view after 6 hours unless it’s checked again. Saved items stay in this browser only and don’t reserve anything. Always confirm price, shipping, and availability on the retailer’s own site before buying.
